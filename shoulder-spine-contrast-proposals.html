Info Panel Contrast Proposals

Three redesign directions for the clinical info sidebar. Current dark scheme (bottom reference) uses cyan/teal/yellow text on near-black — poor contrast and fatiguing. Each option uses the same C6 Radiculopathy content. Dark left strip simulates the anatomy panel.

OPTION A Clinical White
Cervical Radiculopathy
C6 Radiculopathy
C5–C6 Disc Level · Most Common
The most common cervical radiculopathy (~25% of cases). C5–C6 disc herniation compresses the C6 root, causing lateral forearm, thumb, and index finger pain and paresthesia.
C6 Root ACDF Foraminotomy
  • Pain: Neck → shoulder → lateral forearm → thumb/index
  • Weakness: Biceps, brachioradialis, wrist extensors
  • Reflex: ↓ Biceps, ↓ brachioradialis
  • Sensory: Lateral forearm, thumb, index, lateral middle
  • Positive Spurling's sign

White background with navy text. Maximum readability; clinical, clean. Pill tags use light tinted backgrounds — no color bleed. Feels like a medical chart or reference card. Best for readability-first use cases.
