Combo of solids (~2 meals + 1 snack) and formula bottles.

  • Meal ideas: pancakes (cut small, plain or w/ peanut butter), strawberries/blackberries, tomatoes, cucumber, pasta salad, sweet potatoes, sourdough w/ peanut butter, peach cobbler
  • Anything soft, cut into long skinny strips so she can grab it
  • Snacks (bottom pantry bin): rice teething wafers, yogurt melts, puffs
  • Highchair: buckle in, bib on. Plates/utensils/cups in cabinet by fridge, 2nd shelf
DIAPERS

Diapers

  • Change before & after each nap, then check every ~2 hours
  • Diaper rash this week (likely teething-related) — apply Triple Paste at each change
  • Caddies stocked in her room + by downstairs TV. Extra diapers/wipes in garage

Worth a quick check-in with her pediatrician on whether the rash is purely teething or could be diet-related — not urgent, just worth ruling out.

SLEEP

Nap & Bedtime

  • Diaper change in her room — items on dresser in wicker basket
  • Lay open sleep sack on changing table; bedtime pajamas open on top for easy change. No clothes change needed for naps
  • Extra sleep sack (orange) on dresser if needed
  • Close blinds & blackout curtains
  • Sound machine: Hatch — touch metal ring once lightly, fades 1→2 and stays on. Don't tap again
  • Hatch not responding? Go straight to the teal backup (hold center circle to start/stop) — don't keep troubleshooting with a tired baby waiting
  • Pacifiers pre-set in crib, one extra on nightstand
  • Recliner + pillow on lap, hold her facing you, give bottle. She may pass out for an easy crib transfer, or read a few books first
  • Pack-and-play with mattress is available if a change of location is needed
GEAR & EQUIPMENT

Stroller

  • Assembled in front piano room
  • Strap in: pull both arm restraints from behind her like a backpack, pieces join like magnets, clip the leg piece on top to lock
  • To undo: squeeze side buttons on the clamp, pull apart
  • To fold for car: pull the two grey pieces on the handle near the basket toward you

Baby Brezza (Formula)
  • Set ounce amount, place open bottle under spout, press start
  • "Clean" error = rinse the black filter spout under the sink, push back in
  • Check water reservoir in back (like a Keurig)
Bottle Washer
  • Run nightly with the day's bottles — disassemble parts, bottles in bottom rack, parts on grey tray
  • Add a detergent tab (in the clear bin) before closing
  • Check water in back tank
  • Keep the drain hose in the Tupperware container next to the machine
  • Power on → select normal wash and dry → press start
