Anatomy of Expertise

Nerve-Level Precision

Our fellowship-trained surgeons understand hand and upper-extremity anatomy at the nerve-fiber level — from the median nerve's passage through the carpal tunnel to the ulnar nerve's course around the elbow. This depth of knowledge means faster, more accurate diagnoses and targeted treatments.

Median Nerve Carpal tunnel passage · Sensation to thumb, index, middle & radial ring. Source of carpal tunnel syndrome.
Ulnar Nerve Guyon's canal · Pinky and ulnar ring finger. Controls fine motor grip strength.
Radial Sensory Nerve Dorsal branch · Thumb web space and dorsal surface of digits 1–3.
